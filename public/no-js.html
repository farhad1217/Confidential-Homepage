01Why offer a free plan?

Revamp365 charges for optional paid plans and data, AI, and communications usage. The research tools are free, so you can start with property search and analysis and add paid tools when you need them.

02Is the free account a trial?

No. The free plan has no monthly subscription or trial deadline, and you do not need a credit card to create an account. Paid tools and usage charges are separate.

03What can I do first?

Create your account, search a property or market, and open a property record. Review comparable sales and use the flip or rental calculator to explore your numbers.

04What costs extra?

Skip tracing, deep tracing, and AI test calls and chats use credits on Free. List exports, buyer-search tools, CRM workflows, and outbound campaigns require a paid plan. AI and communications usage is billed separately from the subscription.

05Can I search my market without an MLS login?

Yes. You can search nationwide property records and browse available on-market listings through Revamp365. MLS coverage, listing availability, and record detail vary by market.

06How do I get the $5 starter credit?

Create your free account and verify your phone number to claim the credit. Use it toward skip tracing, deep tracing, or AI testing. Credits do not expire; additional usage requires available credits.
